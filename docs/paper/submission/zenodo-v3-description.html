A Six Birds’ Eye View of Dark Energy asks when a homogeneous (FLRW) description of an inhomogeneous universe can be a closed dynamical law in its own right, and what a fitted dark-energy term means when it cannot. Using the lens, completion and packaging vocabulary of Six Birds Theory, the paper proves, with machine-checked Lean 4 proofs, that an exact coarse law exists precisely when micro states with the same observable evolve to states with the same observable (fiber compatibility). Commutation of “coarse-grain then evolve” with “evolve then coarse-grain” is sufficient for closure but not necessary: measured on full states, non-commutation can occur while an exact coarse law exists, whereas measured on the observables it is an exact obstruction.

Two mechanism models make this concrete. For nonlinear evolution observed through a mean, the observable mismatch equals the time step times the nonlinearity times the variance. For Newtonian dust patches that all decelerate, the volume average obeys 3äD/aD = 6 Varw(H) − gρD, so a spread of expansion rates alone can make the average accelerate; an exact two-patch example, checked in Lean, has äD/aD = 5/3. Flat ΛCDM fitted to synthetic distances from such an ensemble, which contains no cosmological constant, returns ΩΛ ≈ 0.60, and a correction shaped by the generator’s heterogeneity fits equally well, although neither fit is statistically adequate.

On public DES SN5YR and DES Y6 BAO data, the provenance-tracked pipeline recovers the published supernova-only parameters, and a correction A(1+z)−m to H2 settles at the boundary m = 0, where it is a cosmological constant. Probe-split runs on DES Y3 and KiDS-450 two-point vectors validate the data layer only. A fundamental cosmological constant is not ruled out; the paper states exactly when a coarse description can close and which audits could separate a closure correction from a fundamental component.

Version 3 rewrites the manuscript for readability and incorporates a mathematical audit of the code and proofs: the exact closure criterion and its Lean formalization, corrected toy-model equations, a BAO likelihood referenced to the release’s template cosmology, multi-start background fits, and figures and tables regenerated from clean, recorded runs.
