1. The exact mathematical target and its proof

Let f:X->Y, U:Y->X, E=U f, and T:X->X. A completion is lawful here when
f(U(y))=y for every y. This is a hypothesis, not a property of arbitrary maps.
It immediately gives E(E(x))=U(f(U(f(x))))=U(f(x))=E(x).

An exact deterministic observable law exists when there is S:Y->Y such that
f(T(x))=S(f(x)) for every x. With a lawful completion this is equivalent to

    f(x)=f(x') implies f(T(x))=f(T(x')) for every x,x'.

Necessity follows by applying the same S to equal arguments. For sufficiency
define S(y)=f(T(U(y))). Since f(U(f(x)))=f(x), fiber compatibility gives
f(T(x))=f(T(U(f(x))))=S(f(x)). Thus the law is constructed, rather than placed
in a hypothesis. An explicit common-fiber pair with distinct evolved readouts
rules out every S, independently of which completion was chosen.

Equivalently, under the same section hypothesis, observable closure is

    E T = E T E.

Fiber compatibility implies this after application of U. Conversely apply f
to the displayed equality, use f U=id, and compare two equal-f states. This
condition is weaker than E T = T E. The latter additionally requires invariance
of the chosen representative states under T.

Counterexample to the manuscript's unrestricted noncommutation implication:
X=Bool x Bool, f(b,c)=b, U(b)=(b,false), T(b,c)=(b,true). Then f T=f, so S=id
is an exact macro law, but E T(b,c)=(b,false) differs from T E(b,c)=(b,true).
Thus a state-space route discrepancy need not be an observable descent
obstruction. A rewrite can repair a chosen representative law, but when fiber
compatibility actually fails, no replacement S on the unchanged observable
space can exactly reconstruct the original evolution for all microstates.
Refinement, additional state/history, approximation, or a modified micro law
is required. A trajectory fit alone is not a proof of autonomous macro closure.

This distinction agrees with HL-META-1 in the repository's knowledge-base
six_birds_theory.tex: quotient evolution descends precisely when it respects
the equivalence relation. The corpus's Holonomy with Memory paper similarly
distinguishes current-compatible transport from a hidden predictive witness.
No imported Six Birds theorem is being used to infer cosmological acceleration.

explicit; NonzeroRouteMismatchAt expresses actual inequality. Historical

2. Toy 1: an exact calculation and the actual reported norm

For n>0, write mu=mean(x), m2=mean(x_i^2), v=m2-mu^2. With
T_i(x)=x_i+dt*(alpha*x_i-beta*x_i^2),

    mean(T(x)) = mu + dt*(alpha*mu-beta*(mu^2+v)),
    T_i(E(x))  = mu + dt*(alpha*mu-beta*mu^2).

Both route outputs are constant vectors, with signed difference -dt*beta*v.
The experiment uses lens-space absolute mean difference, so RM=|dt*beta|*v.
An unnormalized microstate L2 norm would instead give sqrt(n)*|dt*beta|*v.
Idempotence is exact in real arithmetic because the mean of a constant vector
is that constant. Metadata now records the actual mismatch norm separately
from the microstate L2 idempotence readout.

Strict positivity requires dt*beta!=0 AND nonzero variance; nonlinearity alone
does not make the mismatch positive on every state. The observed maxima versus
beta are observations for the configured sweep, not a universal monotonicity
theorem along beta-dependent trajectories. Regression checks exercise the
identity at beta=0 and both signs of beta. Floating equality controls have
roundoff tolerance; they are not interval certificates.

3. Toy 2: normalization, weighting, and acceleration

The actual ODE is ddot a_i=-(g4pi/3)*rho_i0/a_i^2, with
dot a_i(0)^2=(2*g4pi/3)*rho_i0+kappa_i and a_i(0)=1. Its conserved quantity is

    dot a_i^2 - (2*g4pi/3)*rho_i0/a_i = kappa_i,

as direct differentiation shows. Thus H_i^2=(2*g4pi/3)*rho_i+kappa_i/a_i^2.
The coefficient g4pi/3 in the manuscript's local H^2 equation is inconsistent
with its homogeneous dust acceleration equation; the implemented coefficient
2*g4pi/3 is consistent. The code was retained and documented accordingly.

Let V=sum(a_i^3), w_i=a_i^3/V, a_D=(V/N)^(1/3), and H_D=sum(w_i*H_i).
Then dot V/V=3 H_D and

    ddot V/V = 6 sum(w_i H_i^2) - g4pi sum(w_i rho_i),
    3 ddot a_D/a_D = 6 Var_w(H_i) - g4pi rho_D,
    rho_D = sum(w_i rho_i) = sum(rho_i0)/V.

Consequently Q=3 ddot a_D/a_D+g4pi*rho_D = 6 Var_w(H_i)>=0.
rho_D is a volume-weighted density, NOT the unweighted arithmetic mean of
instantaneous densities written in the manuscript's Q definition. Q>0 denotes
departure from homogeneous dust closure; positive acceleration specifically
requires Q>g4pi*rho_D. Local patches remain decelerating. This is an identity
for the proxy ODE and averaging convention; it does not establish an Einstein
solution, relativistic light propagation, a shear contribution, or a physical
GR backreaction magnitude.

Variance is now evaluated as a centered weighted sum, eliminating negative
roundoff values from subtraction of nearly equal second moments. Q is read
from the stable identity; an independent test compares it against the volume
derivative acceleration formula. The RK4 domain is checked at intermediate
states and output states. Nonpositive scale factors are rejected rather than
clipped. The conserved-energy drift is recorded. An analytic homogeneous dust
solution and a step-halving convergence control test the integration separately.
The realized population fraction/density are recorded when rounding f_void*N.

At N=500, dt=.02, steps=1200 and the existing heterogeneous settings:
Q_peak=1.7420340032; late-time positive acceleration fraction=.9875518672.
Maximum relative conserved-energy drift is about 3.15e-9. The Q identity agrees
with the volume acceleration calculation to about 1.4e-14 absolute. These are
finite floating computations, not an exhaustive parameter-range theorem.

4. Synthetic distances and the subtractive rewrite

The generator fits COMOVING distance integral dz/H in units c=1. It does not
multiply by (1+z) to generate luminosity distance, as stated in the methods text.
All fitted synthetic models use the same comoving convention, so their internal
comparison is coherent. Run metadata now identifies that convention. Invalid
scale factors, nonpositive H, duplicate source redshifts, and unsupported
distance extrapolation are rejected instead of clipped or silently edge-filled.

The synthetic rewrite is H^2=H0^2*(1+z)^3-A*g(z), with a MINUS sign. The
public rewrite has an additive correction. The two must not be conflated.
In the synthetic family H0 is the square root of the matter coefficient;
H_present=sqrt(H0^2-A*g(0)), so the old H0 field is not generally a present
Hubble rate. The family and the separate present rate are now reported.

Previous clipping of nonpositive H^2 changed the model and a sampled penalty
did not enforce its full domain. The fit now sets A=fraction*H0^2/R_max,
0<=fraction<=1-1e-8, where R_max=max_[0,zmax] g(z)/(1+z)^3. This maximum is
computed on the ENTIRE piecewise-linear proxy: on g=s*z+b, interior stationary
points are z=(s-3*b)/(2*s), and endpoints are included. This establishes
strict positivity on the interpolation interval for every admissible fit.
The small positivity margin is an explicit numerical restriction. Zero proxy
amplitude is unidentifiable and is rejected. Public evaluation rejects invalid
H^2 rather than using the clipped surrogate. A constructed interior-maximum
counterexample and a noiseless parameter-recovery check test the repair.

The original null-Lambda inference survives: Omega_Lambda=.60150 (seed 123).
At rewrite comparison seed 468, LambdaCDM chi2=379.609 and rewrite chi2=378.593
for 40 data points and two fitted parameters each. Comparable fit quality is
justified; an adequate fit at the stated noise is NOT justified (chi2/38~10).
This is a best-fit point under a misspecified homogeneous flat distance family,
not a posterior detection, a curvature-controlled causal isolation, or a GR
alternative to Lambda. The generator itself contains curvature-like terms.

The three-point delta_rho sweep retains Spearman rho=1. Its old asymptotic
p-value was zero, which is inappropriate for three pairs. Enumerating all six
pairings gives the exact two-sided p-value 2/6=1/3. The ordering is descriptive
and does not establish significance or a universal amplitude law.

5. Synthetic proxy audit: what is independently supplied

The initializer shuffles the locations of exactly the same void and wall
populations. Domain sums are permutation invariant. Thus changing seed at fixed
parameters does NOT create independent heterogeneous expansion histories.
Only the synthetic distance observation noise changes. A regression explicitly
checks this invariance; the experiment records it in its evidence metadata.

The rewrite predictor is a thresholded amplitude ratio times a supplied
generator-variance template. It is not derived from the Friedmann correction.
The template calibration and test histories are identical up to permutation,
and the homogeneous comparator predicts zero variance by its definition.
The median RMSE ratio .008776 (~114x improvement) survives the repair, but
its warranted interpretation is calibrated proxy recovery under repeated
distance noise on the same generator. It does not establish independent
generator generalization or data-only production of a heterogeneity witness.
The calibration input and A_cut=.1 are now visible in run metadata. Parameter
count two applies to the fit conditional on the supplied template, not to the
full calibration process as though the template were free information.

6. Public background likelihoods

The cached DES-SN5YR 1.3 README says STATONLY is zero and STAT+SYS contains
additional systematics; the statistical diagonal comes from MUERR_FINAL.
The loader now follows this specified meaning and never guesses covariance
versus correlation from the magnitude of diagonal entries. A genuine bug
caused triplet parser metadata to overwrite the corrected total covariance
with the raw matrix. The update now excludes the raw cov field. This specific
overwrite did NOT affect the cached release, which uses the flat-vector format;
regressions prove that the supported triplet format also retains the diagonal.
Duplicate zero entries, conflicting symmetric entries, negative indices, and
noninteger covariance sizes are checked.

The supplied SN likelihood uses radial distance at zHD and the luminosity
prefactor (1+zHEL). Previously only zHD was used. The repaired loader exposes
raw mu and mu_cosmological=mu-5*log10[(1+zHEL)/(1+zHD)] separately; runners
compare the latter with the usual mu_th(zHD). This is exactly equivalent to
adding the heliocentric correction to predictions, without changing covariance.
Dataset callers can alternatively supply raw mu and z_hel explicitly.

The intercept formula M_hat=(1^T C^-1 r)/(1^T C^-1 1) is correct. The profiled
chi-square now evaluates the centered residual directly instead of subtracting
two large quadratic terms. Generic information criteria count the profiled
intercept as a fitted parameter; explicit-count and profiled-count fields are
separate. Background reverse audits profile the intercept on the test SN data:
they are shape scores with a refitted intercept, not fully held-out calibrated
distance-vector predictions. Their metadata states this distinction.

ACF, APS, and PCF are correlated statistics from the same DES galaxy sample.
Summing their one-dimensional chi-squares as if independent artificially
sharpens the BAO likelihood. All background/anchor runners now use the released
likelihood_fid consensus curve. The unused independence-combination functions
have been removed. The release README specifies Delta chi-square and
z_eff=.851: neither the sign of a column nor a generic fallback redshift now
determines that meaning. The consensus alpha minimum is .9571 and its
Delta-chi-square-one half-width is .02009994.

Outside the tabulated alpha support, the likelihood returns zero probability
(infinite chi-square) rather than flat edge-filled tails. The optimizer uses a
finite search barrier at unsupported points, but final scores are recomputed
from the actual likelihood and nonfinite results are not reported successful.
Bounded one-parameter fits use a scalar search and explicitly compare endpoints.

The BAO shape-only model fixes H0 to the fiducial value as well as fixing the
sound-horizon ratio. The manuscript's inference that fixed r_d alone removes
H0 dependence is false: D_M scales as 1/H0. The fixed-H0 assumption is now in
evidence metadata. No claim of self-consistent sound-horizon modeling is made.
SN+BAO independence remains a supplied approximation without cross-covariance.

Corrected curved-background fits still give m=0 on SN and on SN+BAO. The latter
has om~.30064, correction~.54736, chi2~1640.411. The extra rewrite parameter adds
Delta AIC~2 with essentially identical fit chi-square. This is the expected
nesting at m=0, not an independent discriminator of the correction's origin.
Flat SN LambdaCDM has om~.35195 and chi2~1640.083 for 1829 entries.

7. Covariance, linear fits, and backend alignment

Gaussian solves now require a finite symmetric positive definite covariance.
There is no automatic eigenvalue clipping or undisclosed jitter ladder; jitter
must be explicitly requested and reported. The cached total SN, DES Y3, and
KiDS matrices factor without it. KiDS no longer reports successful unjittered
validation after an unsuccessful ladder, nor accepts inconsistent duplicates.
Whitened generalized least squares replaces normal equations, avoiding a
squared condition number. Rank-deficient training bases are rejected because
unconstrained directions can produce arbitrary held-out predictions.

Boolean masks select indices rather than being converted into repeated 0/1
indices. Default array distance rejects different shapes instead of permitting
broadcasting between different state spaces. Fixed reference and stub backends
respect original row indices even when requested and full-vector lengths happen
to match; previously an equal-length permutation silently returned wrong rows.
Regression controls exercise each of these cases. Modern pandas parsing was
also repaired so the cached-data checks run in the audit environment.

For an unconstrained linear fit, invertible rescaling of basis columns DOES NOT
change fitted predictions or chi-square. The arbitrary scale of the prescribed
residual injection does change scores. The manuscript should distinguish these
two operations rather than attributing score magnitude to basis normalization.
A basis-unit invariance regression now checks the algebraic fact.

8. LSS protocol scope, retained by user decision

The reference is t0=y+q(C,metadata). Fitting t0+B*p against y yields residual
-q-B*p, so observed central values cancel. The alternative stub similarly
prescribes r0 from covariance and metadata; its two-template extension spans
r0 by construction and can cancel it exactly. Fixed basis before splitting
prevents coefficient-training leakage, but does not make this y-dependent
reference independent of the held-out values. Neither direction, staging,
bootstrap-like subsampling, nor geometry anchoring restores that independence.
The geometry-anchored LSS residual is explicitly -r0*s(theta) and also contains
no measured central-value information beyond covariance and metadata.

These are legitimate injected-residual protocol demonstrations: the mechanics
of masking, fitting, scoring, and provenance are exercised on real covariance
and metadata. They are not empirical cosmological model comparisons or
independent prediction of observed survey vectors. Docstrings and all affected
run bundles now declare audit_kind=injected_residual_protocol,
independent_prediction=false, and empirical_closure_evidence=false. A test
changes measured central values and demonstrates invariance of the scores.

The scored C_BB is a marginal block covariance. A calibrated conditional
Gaussian predictive check would instead use residual
r_B-C_BA*C_AA^-1*r_A and Schur covariance
C_BB-C_BA*C_AA^-1*C_AB, together with parameter uncertainty. The existing point
scores are comparative diagnostics; no calibrated posterior predictive p-value
or universal distinguishability of fundamental Lambda from closure is proved.
Staging signatures can depend on systematics or model misspecification, and
a closure correction can be constant. These signatures are hypotheses requiring
independent data and physical predictions, not consequences of the Lean core.

DES Y3 and KiDS ingestion, both split directions, staging sweeps, anchor runs,
and the robustness suite were freshly exercised. Robustness: 26 successes,
4 insufficient-point skips, 0 failures. Unknown current Y6 online availability
is not a mathematical result: the smoke test now tests local registry blocking
offline and labels that scope, rather than treating absent configuration as a
statement that the collaboration has not released artifacts.
